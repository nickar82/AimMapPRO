AimMap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. AimMap is an Android app by Nickar ("I", "the developer"). Contact: nickar82@gmail.com.

What AimMap does

AimMap shows a camera view and a live map. The map shows a cone from your position in the direction the camera points. It uses your camera, location and the motion sensors of your phone.

Data that stays on your device

  • Camera: the picture is shown on screen only. AimMap does not record video, and does not upload or share camera images.
  • Location, compass and motion sensors: used on your phone to draw the cone and place names. Your position is not sent to me and I have no servers that store it.
  • Saved locations, settings and geo screenshots (Pro): stored on your phone only. Geo screenshots and exports are saved or shared only when you choose to.

Data sent to third parties

  • Map tiles: the map pictures are downloaded from OpenStreetMap, Esri (satellite) or OpenTopoMap servers. These servers receive your IP address and the map area you are looking at.
  • Place names: to show names of towns, villages and peaks, the app sends an approximate area (coordinates rounded to a grid of about 25 km) to the OpenStreetMap Overpass API. Results are cached on your phone.
  • Advertising (free version): the free version shows one full-screen ad when the app opens, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it later. See Google's advertising policy.
  • Purchases: AimMap Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. I do not receive your payment details. Google tells the app whether Pro is owned by your Google account.

Pro removes the ads. I do not sell personal data.

Children

AimMap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

  • You can turn off camera, location or motion permissions in Android settings.
  • You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
  • Ad personalisation can be changed in your Google account and Android settings.
