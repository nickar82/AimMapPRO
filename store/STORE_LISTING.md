# AimMap – Google Play listing (copy and paste)

**App name:** AimMap
**Short description (80 characters max):**
See on a live map exactly where your phone camera is pointing.

**Full description:**
AimMap shows your camera and a live map in one screen. A cone on the map starts at your GPS position and opens in the direction the camera looks. Zoom the camera in and the cone gets narrower, just like the real field of view.

Use it to identify what you are looking at: a peak on the horizon, a village across the valley, a building, a coastline.

FREE
• Camera and live map side by side (portrait) or left and right (landscape), with a draggable divider and a swap button
• Field-of-view cone that follows your camera zoom, runs to the edge of the map at any map zoom, with distance marks
• Names of cities, towns, villages and peaks on the map and on the camera view, with distance and elevation (OpenStreetMap data, needs internet)
• Tap a name on the camera to align the compass to it
• Map, satellite and topographic maps
• Compass calibration helper, units (km/mi, degrees), dark and light themes
• Map can rotate so the cone always points up
• One full-screen ad when the app opens

PRO (one-time purchase, no subscription)
• No ads
• Saved locations with direction, elevation and notes
• Direction lock: lock a bearing and see the deviation live
• Geo screenshots: photos stamped with position, direction, tilt and date
• Choose the cone colour and opacity
• Export and import saved locations (GPX, CSV)

Needs a phone with a compass (magnetometer). Camera, location and motion sensor permissions are used only to show the cone; nothing is uploaded.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
